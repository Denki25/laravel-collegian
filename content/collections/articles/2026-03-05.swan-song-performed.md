            4 graduating CLSU student-artists triumph at reg’l arts events<br><br>
            Despite being deployed in their respective internships, four graduating student-artists from Central Luzon State University (CLSU) achieved placements across various literary and performing arts events in the National Arts Month Celebration hosted by the Culture and the Arts Association of State Universities and Colleges (CAASUC) III, held in Pampanga State Agricultural University, March 5.<br><br>
            Reyniel Tomas, bagged a silver finish in the Dagliang Talumpati competition with his piece anchored on the topic ‘Pagpapanday.’<br>
           
            <blockquote><p>
            “Hindi lang masaya kundi kay gaan sa puso na nagagawa ko pa rin ang magtanghal hindi dahil sa ngalan ng timpalak, kundi dahil mahal ko ang sining at mahal ko ang layunin kumbakit ako nagtatanghal. Kaya anuman ang naging resulta, bunga ito ng pinakaubod kong pag-ibig sa aking ginagawa,” Tomas expressed.
            </blockquote></p>
            <p>

            The silver medalist is a secondary Filipino education intern, who previously won the championship in Spoken Word Poetry (Filipino) during the 2024 Regional Culture and the Arts Festival of Region III (RCAF), and finished 5th in Pagsulat ng Sanaysay last 2025.<br><br>
            On the other hand, Juhaina Mae Bautista (composer) and Jyka Althea Page (interpreter) clinched a bronze medal in the Song Writing competition with their piece entitled “Kultura Nati’y Ipagbunyi.”<br>
            
            <blockquote><p>
            “Actually, sinesendan lang ako ni Sir Alex [Ibarra] ng vocal guides na pinapakinggan ko habang nasa law office ako as an intern. Naka-earbuds lang ako habang nag-mu-multitask ng office tasks at pinapakinggan ang piece,” Page stated, acknowledging her coach, when asked about their preparations. 
            </blockquote></p>
            <p>

            The singer also added that their rehearsals only started two days before the actual competition. <br>
            <blockquote><p>
            "As I reflect on it po, I'm grateful for this opportunity that has given to me, kasi I'm surrounded with talented people in the venue. Kitang-kita po sa kanila ang pagmamahal sa sining at kultura,” Bautista shared as she sums up her experience in the competition.
            </blockquote></p>
            <p>

            Bautista is a sophomore, while Page is a senior accountancy student-intern. Both are part of the CLSU Maestro Singers’ golden finish in the Standard Chorale competition in the regional festival last November 2025, and are part of the singing roster for the nationals.<br><br>
            Furthermore, teaching interns Julius Cunanan and Vince JC Macadangdang both placed 5th in Oratoryo and Extemporaneous Speech, respectively.<br><br>
            Macadangdang is a first-timer in the regional arena of the festival, and performed a speech following the prompt “War” with a ten-minute preparation. Meanwhile, Cunanan who has competed in last year’s regional festival graced the stage with his piece “Lagot Ka!: Kulturang Nilagot ang Diwa ng Kawalang Pananagutan.”<br><br>
            The events served as qualifying stages for the added events in the National Culture and the Arts Festival that were not included in the November 2025 RCAF III competitions. <br><br>
