<p>
            During the height of the 119th Founding Anniversary of Central Luzon State University (CLSU), the University Infirmary recorded approximately 50 student performers who sought medical assistance for fatigue, injuries, and panic attacks.<br><br>
            This number was accumulated on the night of the Festival Dance performances last April 15.<br><br>
            Further, according to Dr. Josephine Fabricante, Medical Officer III of the Infirmary, 31 of these incidents were brought to the University Infirmary for additional check-up due to persisting symptoms.<br><br>
            Most cases reported were panic attacks and heightened emotions caused by pressure and competition anxiety.<br><br>
            </p>
            <blockquote><p>“Halos majority similar [ng symptoms]: hyperventilation, body weakness, difficulty of breathing, highly emotional [...] symptoms of panic attacks,” said Dr. Fabricante.</p></blockquote>
            <p>
            Three performers were given intravenous (IV) hydration for severe dehydration and low blood pressure.<br><br>
            Fabricante said that heavy costumes contributed to the dehydration experienced by the dancers during their performance.<br><br>
            “Lahat sila naka-costume, naka-contribute din [’yon] kaya pagdating nila dito, tinanggal namin yung balot at nilagay sila sa malamig tapos hinydrate sila,” she added.<br><br>
            Moreover, Dr. Fabricante also confirmed cases of fatigue among performers.<br><br>
            “Marami sa kanila hindi kumain, hindi uminom, pagod, at puyat,” she added. “Triggers ’yon sa pagiging anxious nila.”<br><br>
            The CLSU Infirmary also reported sprain injuries, abrasions, and blisters caused by their performance during the competition.<br><br>
            Moreover, the doctor advised students and performers to pursue a healthy lifestyle to avoid health problems especially during major events.<br><br>
            </p>
            <blockquote><p>“Ang precautionary measures [to avoid health problems] kailangan [mag-maintain ng] healthy lifestyle, enough sleep, balanced diet, hydration lalo na ngayon kasi mainit,” said Fabricante.</p></blockquote>
            <p>
            All of them were discharged shortly after their vital signs stabilized, while follow-up consultation was prescribed for patients experiencing further symptoms.
            </p>
