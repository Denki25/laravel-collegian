<p>
            Central Luzon State University (CLSU) logged a 31.51% passing rate in the March 2026 Civil Engineering Licensure Examination (CELE), after 46 of its 146 takers passed, as per the results released by the Professional Regulation Commission on April 7.<br><br>
            This year's result for CLSU is an increase from its previous 17.78% passing rate in the November 2025 CELE.<br><br>
            Forty-four of 101 first-time takers passed, while 2 out of 45 repeaters passed the CELE, leading to 43.56% and 4.44% passing rates for first-time takers and repeaters, respectively.<br><br>
            Moreover, the national passing rate recorded was 35.05% after 6,438 out of 18,370 takers passed the March 2026 CELE.<br><br>
